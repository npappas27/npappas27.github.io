Information We Collect

Personal Data

We may collect the following information when you use Giggy:

  • Email address
  • First and last name (if provided)
  • Usage Data

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically and may include:

  • Device IP address
  • Device type, model, and operating system
  • App version, interaction timestamps, and feature usage metrics

Location Data

With your consent, Giggy may collect precise or approximate location data to display and store gig locations on maps. You can enable or disable this at any time via device settings.

If you choose to upload images (e.g., venue photos), Giggy may request access to your camera or photo library. You can revoke this permission at any time.

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To communicate with you regarding updates or issues

We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties for marketing.

Data Storage and Retention

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, our backend service provider. We retain personal data only as long as necessary to:
