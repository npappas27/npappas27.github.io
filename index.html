CENA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use Cena (the “Application” or “Service”). It also explains your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

By using Cena, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Interpretation and Definitions

Company refers to Cena, operated by Nick Pappas (“We”, “Us”, or “Our”).
Application refers to Cena, the software program provided by the Company.
Device means any device that can access the Service, such as a smartphone.
Personal Data means any information that can identify an individual.
Group means the group of friends you play Cena with inside the Application.
You means the individual accessing or using the Application.

Information We Collect

Account Data

When you sign in with Sign in with Apple, we create an account tied to the identifier Apple provides. We ask you to add:

  • Your name
  • A profile photo (optional)

We do not request or store your email address, even though Sign in with Apple can provide one.

Group and Game Data

Using Cena also creates the data the game itself runs on:

  • The names of groups you create or join, and their invite codes
  • Restaurants picked within a group (name, address, and category, sourced from Apple Maps)
  • Dinner times and notes you add for a pick
  • The scores you and others submit for each dinner

Location Data

With your consent, Cena uses your device's location to bias restaurant search results toward places near you. This happens on your device through Apple Maps; your location is never sent to or stored on our servers. You can enable or disable this at any time via device settings.

Photos and Calendar

If you add a profile photo, Cena requests access to your photo library to let you pick one. If you tap "Add to calendar," the standard Apple calendar composer opens; Cena does not read or store your calendar.

What Your Group Can See

Cena is built for a small group of friends to play together. Your name, photo, picks, and scores are visible to the other members of any group you're part of — that's how the game works. We do not share this information outside your group, and we do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties for marketing. Cena has no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs.

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Application
  • To authenticate you via Sign in with Apple
  • To run the game: pick order, scoring, reveals, and round history
  • To display your groups' history to their members
  • To respond to you if you contact us

Data Storage and Retention

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, our backend service provider, hosted in the United States. We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Data Deletion and Account Removal

You can delete your account at any time from within the Application (Account → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your name and photo, signs you out everywhere, and permanently blocks that account from signing in again. Because a dinner's history and scores belong to everyone in the group who took part, they aren't erased when one member leaves — they stay visible to your groups, attributed to “Deleted user,” instead of being deleted outright. You can also request account deletion by contacting us at njpappas97@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Cena is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including database access rules that limit each group's data to its own members. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at njpappas97@gmail.com.
